Professional Side: Build Status

Checked against the code on 14 October 2026, and compared with the client side. Prepared by the developer.
Headline. The professional side is further along than previously reported. All twenty of its controllers read real records, not sample data. What is missing is not a long list of unbuilt screens. It is one structural gap, two small defects, and four items that are waiting on a decision or an outside service rather than on development time.

1. The one thing that matters most

A professional cannot open an agreement at all. Every agreement route on the platform begins with client/finalize/.... There is no professional route to view an agreement, approve a version, or sign one.

Today the professional's counter signature is written automatically at the moment the client signs, and the code says plainly that this is a pre launch stand in so the flow can be completed in demos. It is labelled as such and was a deliberate choice, not an oversight.

Why it matters now. The Edit Agreement feature just built sends changes "for review". There is currently nobody on the other side to review them. This is the single largest piece of professional side work remaining, and it is the one that unlocks the agreement rules already written.

5. Where the two sides are out of step

The client side has moved a long way recently. These are the places where the professional side has not yet caught up, in the order they affect a real booking.

  • The agreement. A client can now set terms, approve a version, sign, edit the agreement and keep a full version history. A professional can do none of these.
  • Agreement edits. Changes are sent "for review" to a side that cannot review them.
  • Hired Talent and Availability. The client can see who they hired and check availability. The professional has no matching view of who hired them beyond the bookings list.
  • Influencers. The client side now has influencer entries in several places. A client still cannot reach an influencer, because that side is not open.

6. What is extra, and should stay that way

  • Team and Staffing is a professional's own crew and shifts. It is not the retired combined force feature and should not be confused with it.
  • Contracts hub is a bookings pipeline, not the agreement. The two names are close enough to cause confusion later.
  • Food truck and multipurpose stands were never started, so there is nothing to remove. The ordinary food truck booking, where a client hires and pays, continues to work.
Suggested order. First the two small defects, because they are visible and cheap. Then the professional agreement surface, because it is what stops an agreement from ever completing honestly. The four decision items can be answered at any time and do not block the rest.
