Built today, because they were the only two missing

  • Price levels on a package. A professional can offer two or three levels, like Silver and Gold, each with its own price and a line saying what makes it different. The client picks one and is charged that. The price shown on the cards is always the cheapest level, so nobody is advertised a figure they cannot buy at.
  • Check-in on the day. The Gig Operations Hub opens with what is happening today and the professional marks that they are on site. The client sees it and can confirm it. The two are recorded separately, because the professional saying they arrived and the client agreeing are different facts.

Found while checking the other eighteen, and fixed

  • My Packages would not open at all for any professional whose package listed a crew.
  • A package threw away every service the professional chose. They would tick two services, save, and get a package tagged with nothing, which then never appeared in Package Search.
  • Most demo packages could not be found in Package Search, because they were labelled with words that are not services. This is the page that gets demonstrated.
  • Four pages ran off the side of a phone: the Operations Hub, Services & Pricing, Proposals, and Transactions.
  • The Transactions filter row put every filter on its own line, so a row of five looked like five rows.
