3. Two things worth knowing before the answers are set

Charging the professional is a new payment point, not a price change. The platform has never collected money from a Professional or Influencer inside the agreement flow. The $4.99 from their side needs that to be built, and it needs the professional agreement screens, which do not exist yet.
The prices themselves are settings, not code. The amount and the moment it is collected both sit in configuration today, so moving from $2.99 to $4.99, or from finalisation to publish, is a change of setting rather than a rebuild. That is why the answers above matter more than the build.
