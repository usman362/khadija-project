## 1. Things Peter asked for, now done

**Money and fees**

- Posting a request now costs $2.99, charged when it goes live, not at the end.
- The $4.99 agreement fee is separate, and only charged if you actually finalize with someone.
- There is a new page called **Pricing and Rates**. Every charge on the site is on it, in one place.
- Every form now reads its price from that same list, so a price can never be right on one page and wrong on another.
- The "no refunds or credits" wording is now on the pricing page and on the request forms, in the same words everywhere.
- The Starter plan is now free, and people can actually join it.

**The forms**

- You counted eleven questions on the emergency form and eleven on the direct form. Four of those were never required, they just looked the same as the required ones.
- Those optional questions are now folded away behind one line you can open. Both forms now show seven boxes and a Publish button.
- We did not turn them into step-by-step pages. Steps would add clicks without removing a single question, and the emergency form is the one people reach when they are already late.
- A service can no longer run past the time the event ends. That now gets caught as an error, on the main dates and on the backup dates.
- The direct request form was asking for information it had no way to use. That part is now switched off.

**Addresses**

- Both the emergency and the direct forms now ask for a street, a city, and show the state you are served in.
- A street with no city is refused. The same street name exists in several towns, so the city is what tells us which one.
- PO Boxes and nonsense are refused.
- One limit to be honest about: this catches fakes that look wrong. It cannot confirm that a real-looking address actually exists. That needs the paid USPS check, which is written and waiting on the account being set up.

**The verified badge**

- You were right about the legal risk. The badge was showing on people who had never been verified.
- It now only shows when the document is on file and it has been approved.
- The front page was still claiming verification. That is gone.

**Other screens**

- Calendar, Availability and Hired Talent are now three separate pages, as you asked.
- "View as list" works both ways now. You can go back to the calendar.
- The two availability features that did the same thing are now one, with the right name.
- Notifications now go out for messages, for a request going live, for a package purchase, and for every step of an agreement.
- Request Changes is now Edit Agreement, built to your seven rules.

## 2. Things we found ourselves

These were not reported. They were found while doing the work above.

- The approve and decline buttons on an agreement **had never worked**. Not once. They were wired to the wrong place.
- Paid extensions were real money that the accounts could not see at all.
- A repost had no link back to the request it came from, so three attempts at one job looked like three separate jobs.
- The front page was still claiming verification five days after we fixed it, because that text was stored separately.
- Making Starter free would have stopped anyone joining it. We caught that and fixed it in the same pass.
- Four live client accounts were being quoted the wrong posting fee.
- The direct request form had no address checking on it at all. It was the one form missed when addresses were done in October, so the fake address you tried would still have passed there.
- On the direct form, the guest count and venue were being thrown away if the form came back with an error.
